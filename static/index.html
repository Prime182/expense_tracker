Daily habit

Your only recurring job is entering transactions. Everything else — time attributes, trends, comparisons, anomaly flags — is derived automatically every time the page loads.

  1. Hit ＋ Add expense (or press N).
  2. Fill the six fields: Date, Amount, Category, Sub-category, Description, Payment method.
  3. That's it. Every page and metric refreshes on save.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • N — new expense
  • R — reset filters
  • T — toggle theme
  • Esc — close dialog

Filters & drill-downs

The filter bar sits above every page and applies to all of them at once, so switching pages keeps your context. Reset clears everything.

Cross-filtering

  • Click a slice on any category chart to filter to that category.
  • Click a month bar on the Overview or Monthly page to filter to that month.
  • On the Category page, click a category row to drill into its sub-categories, then a sub-category to see the individual transactions behind it.

Anomalies & limits

Anomalies use a median/MAD outlier score against your whole history, so a single large purchase can't hide itself by inflating its own baseline. Limits are optional and never block an entry — they only report status.

Refresh

Data reloads on every save and filter change. Reload the page for a manual refresh.
